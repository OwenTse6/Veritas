My grandmother used to make this soup every time someone in the family got sick, and honestly I think it worked more because of the ritual than any actual medicinal properties, but who am I to argue with results. It's stupidly simple -- just chicken, ginger, scallions, and a splash of rice wine -- but the ginger needs to be a genuinely obnoxious amount or it just tastes like bland broth.

I've tried cutting corners on this recipe a few times and it never works out. Skip the step of toasting the ginger first and you lose like half the flavor. Don't skip it.
