Finished the shelf unit this weekend and I'm pretty happy with how it turned out, even though I definitely made a couple mistakes along the way. Cut one of the side panels about half an inch short and had to redesign the whole bottom section to compensate. Lesson learned: measure twice, then measure again, then cut.

The joints aren't perfect if you look closely but from a few feet away it looks solid. Stained it with a walnut finish which came out darker than I expected on the pine, but I actually like it better this way. Next project is probably a bench for the entryway, assuming I don't run out of wood glue first.
