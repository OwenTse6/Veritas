Okay so I was NOT ready for how good this movie was. Went in expecting a decent popcorn flick and left kind of stunned honestly. The lead performance is genuinely one of the best I've seen this year -- there's a scene near the end that made the whole theater go dead silent, which almost never happens anymore.

That said, it's not perfect. The pacing sags hard in the middle third, and there's a subplot with the sister character that honestly goes nowhere and probably should've been cut. A couple of the jokes land flat too. But even with those issues, the ending more than makes up for it. I walked out still thinking about it two days later, which is more than I can say for most stuff I watch.

If you're on the fence, just go see it. It's messy in places but the good parts are so good they carry the whole thing.
