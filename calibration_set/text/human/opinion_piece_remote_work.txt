I've gone back and forth on remote work more times than I'd like to admit. For the first year it felt like a genuine upgrade to my life -- no commute, more time with family, could throw laundry in during a break. But somewhere around year two I started noticing how isolating it actually is. You stop having those small, dumb hallway conversations that somehow end up mattering more than the scheduled meetings.

I don't think the answer is "back to the office five days a week," that seems like an overcorrection. But I'm less convinced than I used to be that fully remote is obviously better for everyone. Maybe it depends more on the person than the policy.
