Got these headphones on sale and honestly they're fine? Not amazing, not bad. Bass is decent for the price, mids are a little muddy on anything with vocals up front. Battery life is genuinely good though, I get like 30 hours which matches what they claimed, which almost never happens with these companies.

The case feels cheap and the charging port cover already looks like it's going to snap off if I'm not careful. Would I buy them again at full price? No. At the sale price I paid? Yeah probably.
