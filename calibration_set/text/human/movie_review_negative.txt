Went in with low expectations and somehow still left disappointed, which is impressive in a bad way. The plot has maybe three real ideas stretched across two hours, and the dialogue is doing a lot of "as you know" exposition that nobody actually talks like.

That said, the cinematography is genuinely gorgeous in a few scenes, particularly the desert sequence around the hour mark. Not enough to save it though. I'd wait for it to show up free somewhere rather than pay for it.
